# Campus test

The fourth judging criterion asks whether the idea was tested with real users. Nobody has used this build yet; this is a protocol a team can run in an afternoon with a university blockchain club. Report what happened, including what went wrong.

## Who

10 to 20 students currently enrolled at a Turkish university who have used a crypto wallet before. Note who did not finish and why; that is the most useful result.

## Setup

- The app deployed with the Arf line ([deploy.md](deploy.md)), the vault funded with enough devnet USDC for every participant (about 1.5 USDC each), and the treasury with about 0.01 SOL per participant for draw rent and fees.
- Each participant: Phantom or Solflare switched to devnet, 0.2 devnet SOL sent to them beforehand (the faucet is rate limited).
- One observer per two participants, writing down times and where people hesitate. Do not help unless someone is stuck for more than a minute; write down when you did.

## Task

1. Open a line: 0.05 SOL collateral, borrow 1.5 USDC.
2. Cash out 1 USDC as lira.
3. Repay 50 TRY in lira, collateral back.

## Measure

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Finished all three steps | yes / no, and the step where they stopped |
| Time from connecting the wallet to the lira receipt | observer's stopwatch |
| Time for the lira repayment, click to receipt | observer's stopwatch |
| Times the observer had to help | count, with the reason |
| Errors on screen | copy the message |

## Survey (two minutes, after the task)

1. Before today, when you needed lira, did you sell crypto? (never / sometimes / usually)
2. How clear was it that your SOL stays yours while you borrow? (1 to 5)
3. How clear was it where the USDC for your lira repayment came from? (1 to 5)
4. Would you use this with real money if the lira leg were a licensed exchange such as BiLira? (yes / maybe / no, and why)
5. What would stop you?
6. What amount of lira would you typically need, roughly? (under ₺1,000 / ₺1,000 to ₺10,000 / more)
